This project helps save time and makes parking more convenient.

My Travels

Karnataka

Karnataka is a beautiful state in southern India. It is famous for its historical monuments, beautiful hill stations, beaches, waterfalls and wildlife.

Some of my favourite places to visit in Karnataka are:

  • Bengaluru
  • Mysuru
  • Coorg
  • Hampi
  • Chikmagalur
  • Gokarna

I especially enjoy visiting Mysuru for its palaces and Chikmagalur for its scenic mountains and coffee plantations.
